# Cookie Policy

ShipGo uses essential cookies to keep sessions secure, remember language and theme preferences, and complete account or billing flows.

Optional analytics and customer-support tools are enabled only when configured by the operator. These providers may use cookies or similar storage under their own policies.

You can remove non-essential cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent authentication and account features from working.

Contact the site operator through the contact page with questions about this policy.
